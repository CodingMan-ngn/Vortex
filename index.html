About Us

Made by people tired of clutter.

VORTEX started as a weekend fix for a problem we kept hitting ourselves: extensions that ask for too much and explain too little. So we stripped it down — one clear purpose, a UI that stays out of your way, and nothing running that you didn't ask for.

Every release ships small, gets read before it gets merged, and stays local-first by default. No dashboards to log into, no data to hand over. Just the extension, doing the one thing it promises.
